# Fusion Playbook

Use this only if the skill is later merged with another writing or content-safety skill.

## Merge Conditions

- Merge only when both skills share the same primary job: reviewing and rewriting text for harm reduction.
- Do not merge with general polishing, translation, or documentation-writing skills unless inclusive-language review remains a clearly bounded sub-step.

## Preservation Rules

- Preserve the four-part output contract: risk summary, rewritten version, rationale, confirmation items.
- Preserve compatibility guidance for legal, clinical, technical, quoted, and self-identified terms.
- Preserve trigger evals covering direct, indirect, negative, zh, en, and mixed prompts.

## Post-Merge Gate

- Re-run trigger and functional evals.
- Re-check neighbor confusion against humanize-text, technical-documentation-writer, web-search-strategy, and longdoc-evidence-reader.
