# Subtitle style reference

## General tutorial style

- Traditional Chinese sans-serif font with verified glyph coverage.
- White text, dark outline, and restrained shadow.
- Maximum two lines; break by phrase, not fixed character count alone.
- Place captions above phone navigation and social-platform controls.

## Emphasis style

Use one accent color for keywords only. Do not color every word. For karaoke or
word-by-word captions, create ASS subtitles and verify timing against speech at
normal playback speed.

## Bilingual style

- Put the original language on the first line and Traditional Chinese below.
- Keep the two languages visually distinct but readable.
- Translate meaning rather than copying word order.

## Export choices

- SRT: broad compatibility and easy correction.
- VTT: web players and cue settings.
- ASS: advanced position, outline, animation, and per-word styling.
- Burned subtitles: reliable appearance but no longer editable or selectable.
- Soft subtitles: selectable and editable, but appearance depends on the player.
