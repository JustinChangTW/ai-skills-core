# Video Presentation

Turn slides and narration into a paced video while preserving slide fidelity and readable text.

## Workflow

1. Inspect the deck, notes, narration, page count, aspect ratio, and target audience.
2. Use the presentation or PDF workflow to render each slide accurately. Do not reconstruct slides from screenshots when editable source is available.
3. Build a timing sheet with slide number, narration segment, duration, transition, and optional focus area.
4. Match each slide duration to narration plus a small reading buffer. Split long explanations across builds or focus moves instead of rushing.
5. Normalize narration, add optional music below speech, and create subtitles when requested.
6. Render a low-resolution preview, verify page order and sync, then export the final MP4.

## Rules

- Preserve text, charts, logos, and layout from the source deck.
- Do not invent speaker claims, slide content, or citations.
- Keep transitions restrained and consistent.
- Hold dense slides long enough to read; do not animate independent elements simultaneously.
- Use licensed or user-supplied music and imagery.
- Never overwrite the deck, narration, or original render.
