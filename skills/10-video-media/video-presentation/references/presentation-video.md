# Presentation video reference

## Timing sheet

Use columns:

`slide | narration_start | narration_end | visual_duration | transition | focus | notes`

Set visual duration to at least the narration duration plus a short comprehension
buffer. Add longer holds for diagrams, tables, or instructions.

## Rendering

- Render all slides at one consistent frame size.
- Preserve the original aspect ratio with padding rather than distortion.
- Verify fonts before rendering; missing fonts can change line breaks.
- Use high-resolution slide images for zooms.

## Audio

- Remove excessive noise and normalize speech consistently.
- Duck music under narration.
- Avoid changing playback speed merely to force a target duration unless approved.

## Final check

- Slide count and order match the source.
- Narration does not start before the relevant visual.
- Equations, charts, and small text remain legible at final resolution.
- Captions do not cover slide content.
