# Video Short

Create short-form video that preserves meaning instead of merely cutting by duration.

## Workflow

1. Inspect the source and obtain a timestamped transcript when speech drives the story.
2. Identify the target audience, promise, and one main takeaway per clip.
3. Propose candidate clips with source time, hook, context, payoff, and estimated duration.
4. Remove only content that does not support the clip. Do not create misleading statements by rearranging speech.
5. Reframe for the requested orientation. Track the active speaker or subject; never crop faces, hands, captions, or interface targets carelessly.
6. Add readable captions, restrained emphasis, and optional B-roll only when it clarifies the message.
7. Render a preview, check the first 3 seconds and final payoff, then export.

## Defaults

- Use 1080×1920 for vertical delivery unless the user requests another format.
- Keep one idea per clip and front-load the reason to continue watching.
- Preserve at least enough context to avoid changing the speaker's intent.
- Keep captions away from platform controls and the bottom edge.
- Avoid fake engagement claims, fabricated quotations, or unlicensed media.
- Never overwrite source media.
