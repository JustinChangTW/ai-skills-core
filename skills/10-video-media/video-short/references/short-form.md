# Short-form video reference

## Candidate scoring

Score each candidate from 1–5:

- Hook: viewers understand the tension or benefit immediately.
- Completeness: the segment contains enough context and a payoff.
- Clarity: it works without the full source.
- Visual fit: the subject can be framed vertically.
- Integrity: edits preserve the original meaning.

Prefer a slightly longer complete idea over a shorter misleading fragment.

## Pacing

- Remove dead air, repeated attempts, and irrelevant setup.
- Keep intentional pauses that carry emotion or comprehension.
- Use B-roll or punch-ins to clarify, not to hide every cut.
- End after the payoff; avoid long branded tails unless requested.

## Verification

- Watch at normal speed on a phone-sized preview.
- Confirm faces and on-screen text remain inside safe areas.
- Check captions against names, numbers, and technical terms.
- Compare the final statement with the source transcript for meaning.
