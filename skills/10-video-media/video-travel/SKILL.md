# Video Travel

Build a travel story from real media while keeping dates, places, and people accurate.

## Workflow

1. Inventory clips and photos. Preserve originals and capture usable date/orientation metadata.
2. Ask for or infer only a provisional story structure: chronological days, destinations, or a theme. Clearly mark uncertain locations.
3. Create a selects list based on focus, stability, emotion, uniqueness, and story value.
4. Build a sequence with an opening, day/location chapters, human moments, key sights, and a concise ending.
5. Add verified place names, dates, short captions, and optional map cards. Do not fabricate routes.
6. Normalize mixed phone formats, stabilize only when beneficial, balance sound, and use licensed music.
7. Produce a preview for family/name/location review before final export.

## Rules

- Do not expose booking numbers, passports, addresses, QR codes, license plates, or children's personal information.
- Ask before using identifiable faces in public-facing output.
- Strip unnecessary location metadata from share copies.
- Keep original ambient sound when it adds meaning.
- Avoid excessive transitions and random effects.
- Never overwrite source photos or clips.
