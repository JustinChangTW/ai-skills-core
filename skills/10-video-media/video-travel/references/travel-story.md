# Travel video reference

## Selects criteria

Rate each item for:

- Technical usability: focus, exposure, stability, sound.
- Story value: establishes place, shows an action, reveals emotion, or closes a beat.
- Uniqueness: avoid five nearly identical clips.
- Privacy: safe to share with the intended audience.

## Story structures

- Day-by-day: best for complete trip records.
- Destination chapters: best when several places are visited.
- Theme: food, family, shopping, transport, or scenery.
- Highlight recap: short, music-led, minimal explanation.

## Title cards

Use verified Traditional Chinese and local-language names. Keep date formats
consistent. For maps, use a verified route or label the graphic as schematic.

## Delivery

- Archive master: highest practical source resolution.
- Family/TV: 16:9 H.264/AAC MP4.
- Phone/LINE: compressed MP4 with large captions.
- Short social recap: 9:16 with a separate review for public privacy.
